CityPulse

Help & FAQ

Everything you need to know about CityPulse.

How do I register for an event?

Go to the Register section, fill your details, pick an event and click Register Now.

Can I save my preferences?

Yes! Check "Remember my event preference" — it saves to localStorage automatically.

How do I find nearby events?

Click "Find Nearby Events" on the home page and allow location access.

How do I submit feedback?

Visit the Feedback section, select the event, rate it, write your comment and submit.
