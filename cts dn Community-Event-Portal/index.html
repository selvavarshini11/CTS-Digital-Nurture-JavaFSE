Community Bulletin

🏆 Sports Day Recap

Last Saturday's Community Sports Day brought together over 400 residents at Riverside Park. Participants of all ages competed in friendly races, tug-of-war, and cricket matches. Winners received trophies and gift vouchers sponsored by local businesses.

🎨 Art Exhibition Opens

The annual Local Art Exhibition opened its doors at Town Hall Gallery this week. Featuring over 80 works by emerging artists from the neighbourhood, this year's theme is "Urban Roots." The exhibition runs until the end of the month and admission is free for residents under 18.

🍕 Food Festival Highlights

The Food Festival drew record crowds this year with 60+ stalls representing cuisines from across the globe. Community favourites won top honours. Next year's festival is already being planned — applications for vendors open next month.

🎵 Concert Coming Soon

The City Council has confirmed the Summer Music Concert for the last weekend of next month at Central Park. Local bands will take the stage over two evenings. Registration opens this Friday — seats are limited so early sign-up is encouraged.
